## Senior Backend Engineer – PayPay

### About PayPay

PayPay is one of Japan’s largest mobile payment platforms, serving tens of millions of users. We build highly scalable, reliable, and secure payment systems that operate at massive scale. Our engineering culture emphasizes ownership, system reliability, and continuous improvement.

---

### Responsibilities

* Design, develop, and operate **highly scalable backend systems** for payment and financial services.
* Build and maintain **distributed, high-throughput, low-latency systems** that handle critical financial transactions.
* Lead system design discussions with a strong focus on **availability, consistency, and fault tolerance**.
* Ensure **data correctness and transaction safety** through idempotency, state machines, and reconciliation mechanisms.
* Collaborate closely with product managers, SREs, and other engineering teams to deliver reliable solutions.
* Improve system performance, observability, and operational stability.
* Review code and provide technical guidance to other engineers.

---

### Required Qualifications

* **5+ years of backend engineering experience** in large-scale systems.
* Strong proficiency in **Java (or equivalent JVM-based languages)**.
* Solid understanding of **distributed systems**, microservices architecture, and RESTful APIs.
* Experience with **high-concurrency and high-availability** system design.
* Hands-on experience with **relational databases** (MySQL/Aurora) and **NoSQL / caching systems** (Redis, DynamoDB, etc.).
* Deep understanding of **transaction management, idempotency, and eventual consistency**.
* Familiarity with **cloud platforms** (AWS or GCP).
* Strong problem-solving skills and ownership mindset.

---

### Preferred Qualifications

* Experience in **payment, fintech, or financial systems**.
* Experience with **message queues / streaming platforms** (Kafka, Pub/Sub).
* Knowledge of **system observability** (metrics, logging, tracing).
* Experience operating systems in **production at scale**.
* Ability to communicate effectively in **English** (Japanese is a plus, but not required).
